About CalcNest

CalcNest is a small, independent project with a simple mission: free online calculators that explain themselves.

Too many calculator websites return a number and leave you wondering: Is this right? What does it actually mean? Should I trust it?

Every tool on CalcNest follows the same three-part structure:

  • The calculator — accurate, standard math that runs entirely in your browser.
  • The formula — the exact equation used, shown openly so you can verify it.
  • The explanation — a plain-English interpretation of what your result means for your situation.

What we build

We focus on a small set of high-impact financial tools that people actually search for:

  • Rent affordability
  • Retirement savings
  • Credit card payoff
  • Compound interest

We deliberately keep the catalogue small. We would rather ship four well-tested, clearly explained tools than forty rushed pages.

Privacy

All calculations run on your device. You can verify this by disconnecting from the internet and using any tool — everything still works. We do not collect or store the numbers you enter. See our Privacy Policy for details.
